cards for naming
what we feel

we are emotional beings

human beings are interesting. we move through a whole spectrum of emotions, often several at once, and most of them never get a name. when we can name a feeling, we can sit with it instead of running from it. these 48 cards are small doorways.

  1. pick a cardwhen you feel stuck, curious or overwhelmed.
  2. read it slowlythe emotion side speaks in its own voice. notice what opens.
  3. scan the cardpoint your camera at the front with the logo. you’ll see what the feeling means, a prompt to write about, and a small activity to try.

may these cards help you become aware of yours, the way they’re helping us.

not a clinical tool. these cards are for reflection and emotional awareness.
