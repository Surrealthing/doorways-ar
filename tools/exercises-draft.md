# Doorways · exercises, draft 4

One per emotion. Kinds: **breath** · **sensory** (spot sounds, textures) · **task** (a simple action to ground) ·
**words** (affirmations, poems, proverbs) · **inquiry** (riddles, reflective questions) ·
**media** (songs to pick). Nothing here needs a picture, video or puzzle shown in the app.
Mix: task 10 · words 9 · inquiry 9 · breath 8 · sensory 8 · media 4.
Draft 2 (move / make / notice…) is kept in exercises-draft-2.md. `timer` in seconds.
